The marketing question

Which customer action should a campaign support?

For a wholesale-banking digital-marketing discussion, the starting point could be a business customer discovering a relevant service, requesting a conversation or progressing to an agreed milestone. The measures below are proposals for joint definition—not a statement of Juan’s actual scorecard or OCBC’s current performance.

Proposed measureHow we could define itWhat to avoid assuming
Qualified-enquiry rateEnquiries meeting jointly agreed service, company-fit and intent criteria ÷ eligible contacts in the evaluated audience.A form submission or email click is not automatically a qualified opportunity. Sales acceptance requires the relevant team’s input.
Enquiry progressionQualified enquiries reaching an agreed next milestone within a defined window ÷ qualified enquiries.Progression needs reliable CRM status signals; it cannot be inferred from email engagement.
Re-engagement to useful actionPreviously inactive eligible contacts completing the agreed action during the evaluation window.A return visit does not establish purchase intent or a new sales opportunity.
Execution and contact qualityPreparation time, manual handoffs, suppression accuracy, opt-outs and complaints alongside customer outcomes.Faster campaigns or additional messages are not success measures by themselves.
Where sample size and the sales cycle permit, a comparison group can help assess incremental contribution. Revenue or pipeline reporting would require agreed attribution, CRM linkage and sales ownership—not a claim that marketing messages caused every downstream outcome.
Potential use cases

One service. One audience. A relevant next step.

These illustrative journeys are not confirmed OCBC initiatives. Choose a service within the team’s remit and validate the audience, permissions, data and desired action before proposing implementation.

Service interest → relevant enquiry

An eligible contact explores business-service information but has not requested a conversation.

Potential support

Use the observed topic to shape useful educational communication, then stop the corresponding prompts once an enquiry is recorded.

Measure

Qualified enquiries and accepted handoffs, separately from content clicks.

Content engagement → clearer intent

A permissioned contact engages with an educational campaign or event on a relevant business topic.

Potential support

Offer a related explanation or a clear route to speak with the appropriate team, rather than repeating a generic product message.

Measure

Meaningful requests and agreed progression milestones—not attendance or opens alone.

Changed interest → updated journey

A contact’s interests, enquiry status or relationship stage changes during a campaign.

Potential support

Update eligible segments and journey paths, with suppression and relationship-manager handoffs where the relevant signals are available.

Measure

Relevant progression, duplicate-message avoidance and contact pressure.

An example to discuss—not a deployed design

From interest to a coordinated handoff.

01 / SIGNAL

Recognise relevant interest

A known, eligible business contact engages with information on a selected service. Confirm what the event actually signifies.

02 / CONTEXT

Check the relationship stage

Use available identity, permission and enquiry-status signals. Keep account-level context distinct from individual-contact behaviour.

03 / ENGAGEMENT

Offer useful guidance

Provide an appropriate next step within agreed contact rules. Exit the sequence when its intended action is complete.

04 / HANDOFF

Coordinate with the team

Where technically supported, reflect a completed enquiry in the journey and the agreed handoff process. Track acceptance and subsequent progression.

Timing depends on event availability, integrations and operational requirements. This brief makes no latency, conversion-uplift or integration-effort guarantee.

What this means for the discussion

The case provides a banking example of behaviour-led communication. It does not establish a qualified-enquiry, wholesale-banking pipeline or revenue uplift for OCBC.

A relevant OCBC evaluation would define its own downstream customer action and test whether more contextual communication contributes to it.

Vendor-published evidence. Results belong to RCBC and are not a forecast, guarantee or direct quotation from OCBC.

Fit with existing operations

Scope the dependencies—not a platform switch.

No replacement of core banking systems is proposed. Whether a complementary engagement use case is practical depends on the existing architecture and ownership model.

Data and technical validation

  • Contact identity and any permitted company/account relationships.
  • Event definitions, enquiry-status signals and update frequency.
  • Integration boundaries and the system of record.
  • Data access, residency, retention and security review.

Marketing and business controls

  • Permissions, eligibility and suppression rules.
  • Approved content and human review responsibilities.
  • Relationship-manager and sales handoff ownership.
  • Qualification criteria, measurement window and comparison design.

No assumption is made about OCBC’s current platform, data gaps, integration readiness or compliance approval. Any proposal would require the appropriate internal reviews.
